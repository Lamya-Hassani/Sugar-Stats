The Laboratory

We don't just bake; we calibrate. At Sugar & Stats, every gram of flour and degree of heat is measured to ensure consistent euphoria.

Our "Stats" aren't just numbers—they are the exact temperatures required to release the oils in rosemary, or the precise hydration needed for the perfect sourdough crumb.

Our Story

Founded in 2024 in the heart of Casablanca, Sugar & Stats was born from a simple question: Can science make food taste like love?

We replaced "a pinch of this" with "0.05 grams of that." The result is a bakery where nostalgia meets precision engineering.
